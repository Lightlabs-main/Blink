How it works

From a QR code to a share

1

A creator funds a drop

They choose the stock, the amount per person and who can take part, then approve it from their own wallet.

2

You open it in Blink

Scan the QR or tap the link. Sign in with your email and Blink creates a Solana wallet for you — no seed phrase.

3

Settlement is verifiable

When you qualify, the stock arrives and you get a receipt with a Solana transaction you can check yourself.

“Your stock stays in an account you own. Blink can only distribute the amount you approve.” How creators stay in control
For creators

Give stock away. Keep control.

Pick a stock, set the amount per person, fund it with one approval in your wallet, and share the link or QR.

  • Stock moves into a campaign account you own, never into a Blink wallet.
  • You approve an exact amount. Blink can distribute that much and not a single share more.
  • Each drop gets its own distribution key, so one drop can never touch another.
  • You can close a drop at any time and take back what hasn’t been claimed.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Sign in with your email and Blink creates a Solana wallet for you. Rewards and gifts arrive there, and there is no seed phrase to write down.

Is claiming free?

Yes. Blink covers the network fee for reward transfers. Creators pay to set up and fund their own drops, and SKR tips and boosts are paid from the sender’s own wallet, with the amount and fee shown before you approve.

Is this real stock?

xStocks are issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited. They are tokenized tracker certificates that give economic exposure to the underlying stock. They are not registered shares and carry no shareholder voting rights. Blink runs on Solana mainnet, so rewards are real xStocks and their value moves with the underlying stock.

Who can receive xStocks?

xStocks are not available in the United States or to U.S. persons, in the United Kingdom, or in a number of other countries listed by the issuer. Before you receive xStocks, Blink asks for your country and checks the country of your connection. It keeps only the result, never your address, location or ID.

What happens when I deploy on the ORE board?

You approve each deploy in your own wallet. The SOL you deploy is committed to the round, and depending on the outcome you may lose it or get back only part of it. It isn’t a network fee, and Blink doesn’t promise any ORE rewards.

How do creators stay in control?

Stock stays in an account the creator owns. They approve an exact amount Blink may distribute, can close the drop at any time, and each drop has its own distribution key.

How is Tap Rush kept fair?

Rounds are timed by Blink’s server, and each tap’s timing is checked for human patterns. Everyone can earn once per drop.

Which phones are supported?

Blink is an Android app, built with the Solana Seeker in mind. Download the APK from this site and install it.
