Blink-to-Stock
The social distribution network for tokenized stocks

Stocks you can share.

A friend, creator or business drops real tokenized stock as a link or QR code. Open it in Blink, play Tap Rush or claim, and the stock lands in your own wallet on Solana. No seed phrase, no exchange, no fees.

Download the Android app

How it works

1

A creator funds a drop from their own wallet. The stock stays in an account they own.

2

They share a link or QR. You open it in Blink and sign in with your email.

3

Claim it, win it in Tap Rush, or get it with a friend's invite. It's sent straight to your wallet.

Blink determines campaign eligibility; asset settlement is transparent and verifiable on Solana. Currently running on Solana devnet with test stock that has no real value.
