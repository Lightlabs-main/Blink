Page one · Consumer finance

Tokenized stocks, now programmable.

Set the conditions. Create the action. Fund it with stock. Share it anywhere. Blink turns xStocks into social campaigns that communities take part in together.

By the Blink-to-Stock desk3 min read

Getting a tokenized stock has, until now, meant a wallet, a seed phrase, a crypto exchange and a working knowledge of token standards. Blink-to-Stock replaces all of that with a single scan.

A creator, community or business decides who can join — say, verified Seeker owners or SKR stakers — and what they must do, such as winning a ten-second Tap Rush. They fund the campaign with stock from their own Solana wallet and share it as a link or QR code.

Participants sign in with an email address. Blink checks every requirement onchain, and those who qualify receive the reward on Solana, with a public receipt. Then they challenge a friend to do the same.

Creators never hand their stock to Blink. It stays in an account they own, and Blink may distribute only the exact amount they approve.

Section E · Opinion

Give stock away. Keep control.

“Your campaign stock stays in an account you own. Blink can only distribute the amount you approve.” — The creator's guarantee

Run a campaign from your own Solana wallet: pick a stock, set the amount per person, fund it with one signature, share the QR.

  1. Your stock moves into a campaign account you own — never into a Blink wallet.
  2. You approve an exact amount. Blink can hand out that much, and not a single share more.
  3. Each campaign gets its own distribution key, so one campaign can never touch another.
  4. Revoke from your wallet at any time. Blink notices and pauses the drop before the next payout.

Section H · Q&A

Your questions, answered

Do I need a crypto wallet to claim?

No. Sign in with your email and Blink creates a secure Solana wallet for you. Rewards are sent there automatically.

Is it really free?

Yes. Claiming costs you nothing — Blink covers the network fees for reward transfers. Creators pay for setting up and funding their own campaigns.

Is this real stock?

Blink distributes xStocks, issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited: tokenized tracker certificates that give economic exposure to the underlying stock. They are not registered shares and carry no shareholder voting rights. This test edition runs on Solana devnet with a test asset that has no real value.

Who can receive xStocks?

xStocks are not available in the United States or to U.S. persons, in the United Kingdom, or in a number of other countries listed by the issuer. Before you receive xStocks, Blink asks for your country and checks the country of your connection. It keeps only the result — never your address, location or ID.

How do creators stay in control?

Campaign stock stays in an account the creator owns. They approve an exact amount Blink may distribute, can revoke it at any time, and each campaign has its own distribution key.

How is Tap Rush kept fair?

Rounds are timed by Blink's server and each tap's timing is checked for human patterns. Everyone can earn once per drop, with a limited number of tries.

Which phones are supported?

Blink is an Android app, designed with the Solana Seeker in mind. Download the APK from this site and install it on your phone.
