How it works

From a QR code to real stock in seconds

Normally, owning a tokenized stock means a crypto wallet, a seed phrase, buying SOL and finding an exchange. Blink replaces all of that with one scan.

01

A creator drops stock

A friend, creator, community or business funds a drop from their own Solana wallet and shares it as a link or QR code.

02

You open it in Blink

Scan the code or tap the link. Sign in with your email — Blink creates a secure wallet for you automatically.

03

The stock is yours

Claim it, win it or earn it with a friend. The stock is sent straight to your wallet, with a receipt you can check on Solana.

For creators

Give away stock without giving up control

Run a campaign from your own Solana wallet. Pick a stock, set how much each person gets, fund it with one signature and share the QR.

  • Your campaign stock stays in an account you own — never in a Blink wallet.
  • You approve an exact amount. Blink can distribute that much and not a single share more.
  • Each campaign has its own distribution key, so one campaign can never touch another.
  • Revoke the approval from your wallet at any time; Blink pauses the drop automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a crypto wallet to claim?

No. Sign in with your email and Blink creates a secure Solana wallet for you. Rewards are sent there automatically.

Is it really free?

Yes. Claiming costs you nothing — Blink covers the network fees for reward transfers. Creators pay for setting up and funding their own campaigns.

Is this real stock?

Blink distributes xStocks, tokenized shares on Solana. The current test version uses Solana devnet and a test stock with no real value while the app is being evaluated.

How do creators stay in control?

Campaign stock stays in an account the creator owns. The creator approves an exact amount that Blink may distribute, can revoke it at any time, and each campaign uses its own distribution key.

How is Tap Rush kept fair?

Rounds are timed by Blink's server, and each tap's timing is checked for human patterns. Each person can earn once per drop, with a limited number of tries.

Which phones are supported?

Blink is an Android app, designed with the Solana Seeker in mind. Download the APK from this site and install it on your phone.
