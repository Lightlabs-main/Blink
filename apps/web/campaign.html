How to claim
  1. Install Blink for Android and open this link again — it opens straight in the app.
  2. Sign in with your email. A secure wallet is created for you.
  3. Claim it — the stock is sent to your wallet for free.

The creator's stock stays in an account they own. Blink can only distribute the amount they approved. Every reward is a public Solana transaction. xStocks are issued by a third party, which can freeze or move them in any wallet. Blink runs on Solana mainnet: rewards are real xStocks.
